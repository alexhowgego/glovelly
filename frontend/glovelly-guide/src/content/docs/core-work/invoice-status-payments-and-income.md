## Keep an eye on invoice status

<a class="menu-link" href="https://menu.glovelly.net/workspaces/invoices">Open Glovelly Menu to check invoices</a>

Use the **Invoices** workspace to filter invoices by drafts, outstanding invoices, overdue invoices, and paid invoices. Open an invoice to preview its PDF, review its line items, or take the next available status action.

## Mark an invoice paid

1. Open the issued or overdue invoice.
2. Choose the action to mark it **Paid**.
3. Confirm once payment has arrived.

Glovelly records the payment date using the current UK date.

## Drafts, reissuing, and cancellation

Draft PDFs have a **DRAFT** watermark. Change the status to **Issued** to create a clean PDF; this does not send it. If you re-issue an invoice, it returns to Draft and its replacement PDF is watermarked until you issue it again.

When you select Issued, one confirmation lets you optionally mark linked gigs completed. Choose **Cancel** to leave everything unchanged. Returning a Cancelled invoice to Draft restores its watermarked PDF automatically; if regeneration fails, use the inline **Try again** action.

Cancelling a draft that was never issued does not remove the watermark. A cancelled invoice that was already issued can still have a clean PDF. Copies you previously downloaded, emailed, or published to Drive do not change when you update the invoice in Glovelly.

Cancelled invoices cannot be emailed or published through Glovelly, even if their saved PDF is clean. Restore them to Draft, then issue before delivery. Sending or publishing a Draft always issues it first; there is no option to deliver a watermarked draft through the app.

## See paid income for the financial year

The **Income this financial year** view includes paid invoices received between 6 April and 5 April. It is a useful record of paid income, not tax advice or a tax return.

This summary and its matching invoice filter require Solo or Pro. Free still includes payment recording, ordinary paid-invoice filters, and complete invoice history. See [plans and invoice allowances](/set-up/plans-and-allowances/) for first-issue limits and what happens when paid access ends.
