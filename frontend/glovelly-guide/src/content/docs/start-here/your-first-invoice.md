## The outcome

Create a client and a gig, add any costs you need to claim, then generate and review an invoice before sending it.

## What you need

- the client's name, billing email, address line 1, and city;
- the gig title, location, date, and agreed fee; and
- any travel or receipts you want to keep with the work.

<a class="menu-link" href="https://menu.glovelly.net">Open Glovelly Menu to start your invoice</a>

## Do this next

1. [Add the client](/core-work/clients/). Include the email address that should receive invoices.
2. [Create the gig](/core-work/gigs/) and choose the client, type, date, location, fee, and status.
3. If you had costs, [add expenses, receipts, or mileage](/core-work/expenses-receipts-and-mileage/).
4. Generate an invoice from the saved gig.
5. [Review the invoice and email](/core-work/create-review-and-send-invoices/) before choosing **Send invoice**.

## What happens next

The gig is linked to the invoice. You can preview or download the PDF, track its status, and [mark it paid](/core-work/invoice-status-payments-and-income/) when payment arrives.
