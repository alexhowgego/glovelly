title: Sign in or request access
description: Use the right Google account to open Glovelly or request access.
---

## Sign in with the account linked to Glovelly

1. Open [Glovelly Menu](https://menu.glovelly.net).
2. Select **Continue with Google**.
3. Choose the Google account linked to your Glovelly invitation.

Google sign-in confirms who you are. It does not, by itself, create a Glovelly account.

## If access is not enabled yet

After signing in with Google, Glovelly can offer **Request access** when that account is not yet enabled. Submit the request from that screen and wait for its outcome before trying again.

:::note
Use the same Google email address that your invitation was sent to. A successful sign-in with a different account will not open the invited account.
:::

If access is not available yet, send a request and check back later using the same Google account.
