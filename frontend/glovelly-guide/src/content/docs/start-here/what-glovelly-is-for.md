## The job around the job

A gig is rarely just a gig. There is a client to remember, a fee to agree, travel to record, a receipt to keep, and often an invoice to send afterwards. Glovelly gives those details one practical home.

Use Glovelly to:

- keep client contact and billing details nearby;
- record planned, completed, cancelled, or draft gigs;
- keep expenses, receipts, and mileage with the relevant gig;
- create invoice PDFs from your work and review them before sending; and
- see which invoices are still outstanding or have been paid.
