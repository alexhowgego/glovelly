## Open your settings

Open the profile menu and choose **Your settings**. You can update your display name and defaults that save time when you create future clients, gigs, and invoices.

## Useful defaults

Set defaults for things you use regularly, such as:

- travel origin postcode and mileage rates;
- default invoice payment window;
- invoice filename and email-subject patterns;
- invoice email message and reply-to address; and
- an invoice upload folder when you use Google Drive.

Defaults help with new work. They do not silently rewrite existing clients, gigs, or invoices.

:::tip
Make a small test invoice after changing an invoice default so you can check the resulting PDF and message before the next real delivery.
:::
