---
title: Plans and invoice allowances
description: Check your plan, understand first issuance, and keep working when an allowance is used.
---

Open **Invoices** to see your current plan, this month's first-issued invoice count, and the next reset time.

## What uses an allowance unit?

On Free, you can first-issue **two invoices per calendar month**, using UK time. Creating, editing, previewing, or downloading a watermarked draft does not use a unit.

The existing issue confirmation or email/Drive review explains the allowance before you continue. First issuance removes **DRAFT** from the PDF; issuing alone does not send it.

Correcting and issuing the **same invoice again** uses no additional unit, even in a later month. Delivery retries do not use another unit. Cancelling or deleting an invoice does not give its unit back.

Unused allowance does not carry into the next month. Solo and Pro currently include unlimited issuance and the financial-year paid-income summary. These plan differences are provisional; there is no online checkout.

## If your allowance is used

Keep preparing drafts and downloading their watermarked PDFs. Wait for the displayed UK-month reset, or contact a licence administrator about changing your plan.

All your records and history remain available. You can still record payments and use existing downloads and exports. The paid-income summary is a Solo/Pro reporting convenience; ordinary paid-invoice records are not hidden on Free.

## If paid access ends

A manual paid grant may be permanent or have an end date. When it expires or is revoked, you fall back to Free without losing records.

Your usage continues across plan changes. If you first-issued more than two invoices on Pro earlier this month, changing to Free does not give you two fresh units. Previously issued invoices remain correctable without using another unit.

Existing eligible users received permanent Pro access with the reason **Founder** at the one-time rollout. Signing in for the first time afterward does not create a Founder grant.

## For licence administrators

If you have **ManageLicences**, open **Licences** and select a user from the directory to review their plan, usage, and history. Choose **Edit licence** to open the Management Pane, then choose a plan and optionally set a future UTC end date. Leave expiry blank for permanent access.

Add a short reason and choose **Save and close** to return to the refreshed overview, or **Save** to keep editing. **Discard changes** leaves their saved assignment unchanged.

Use **Recent licence changes** to review who changed access and what changed. If an assignment changed while you were editing, reload and select the user again before saving.

Licensing does not grant account-management or permission-management authority. A plan change never resets usage or promotes an administrator.
