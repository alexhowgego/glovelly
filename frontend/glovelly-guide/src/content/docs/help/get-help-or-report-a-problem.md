## Need help using Glovelly?

<a class="menu-link" href="mailto:support@glovelly.net">Contact help</a>

Email [support@glovelly.net](mailto:support@glovelly.net) when you need help completing a task, understanding what you see, or getting back to work.

## Something broken or unexpected?

<a class="menu-link" href="https://github.com/alexhowgego/glovelly/issues/new">Report a problem</a>

Use the problem report route when Glovelly does something unexpected, fails to save, shows an error, or gives you a result that does not look right.

## Make a report useful

Tell us:

- what you were trying to do;
- what happened instead;
- the approximate time it happened; and
- a screenshot, if it does not expose sensitive information.

Do not send passwords, payment card details, or more client/personal information than is needed to understand the problem.

:::tip
If a problem stops an invoice or another time-sensitive job, say so in the first line of your message. It helps us understand the impact quickly.
:::
